# 🔐 PrivScope — Social Media Privacy Risk Assessment

**Understand your digital exposure. Protect your online identity.**

PrivScope is a web-based cybersecurity project designed to help users evaluate their social media privacy risks, understand their digital footprint, and discover practical ways to improve their online security.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://socialmediaprivacyriskassessment.lovable.app

## 📌 Project Overview

Social media platforms collect and expose different types of personal information. Weak privacy settings, excessive sharing, location exposure, third-party applications, and poor account security can increase online privacy risks.

PrivScope provides a structured self-assessment framework that helps users identify potential privacy vulnerabilities through a questionnaire and generates a personalized exposure report.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📋 **44-Question Assessment:** Evaluate privacy settings and online habits.
* 📊 **Privacy Exposure Score:** Get a score from 0 to 100.
* 🔍 **Category-Based Analysis:** Review risks across 10 assessment categories.
* 🚨 **Risk Classification:** Identify low, moderate, high, and critical exposure.
* 🛡️ **Actionable Recommendations:** Receive prioritized suggestions to improve privacy.
* 📝 **Fix-It Checklist:** Follow practical steps to reduce exposure.
* 📱 **Interactive Dashboard:** Review your assessment results.
* 🔒 **Privacy-Focused Design:** The framework uses answers entered by the user and is designed to keep assessment data in the user's browser.

## 🧠 Assessment Categories

1. Profile Visibility
2. Personal Information
3. Location Privacy
4. Posts & Content
5. Friends / Followers
6. Tagging & Mentions
7. Authentication & Account Security
8. Third-Party Apps
9. Messaging & Social Engineering
10. Digital Footprint

## 📊 Risk Classification

| Exposure Score | Risk Level  |
| -------------- | ----------- |
| 0–20           | 🟢 Low      |
| 21–40          | 🟡 Moderate |
| 41–70          | 🟠 High     |
| 71–100         | 🔴 Critical |

A higher score indicates greater potential privacy exposure. The score is an educational self-assessment, not a prediction of whether an account will be compromised.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Questionnaire:** Users answer questions about privacy settings and online habits.
2. **Validation:** Responses are checked for completeness and validity.
3. **Feature Extraction:** Relevant privacy-risk indicators are identified.
4. **Category Analysis:** Responses are organized into privacy and security categories.
5. **Scoring Engine:** A risk score is calculated from the assessment.
6. **Classification:** The exposure score is mapped to a risk level.
7. **Recommendations:** Prioritized privacy improvement suggestions are generated.
8. **Report:** Users review their exposure score, findings, and fix-it checklist.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

PrivScope is designed as a defensive, educational privacy assessment tool.

* Assessment responses are entered by the user.
* The published framework states that assessment data stays in the user's browser.
* The tool does not scrape social media profiles.
* Users should avoid entering passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials into any assessment.
